    Selected Working Papers & Ongoing Projects

    Well-Posed Reformulation of Bilevel Programming
    Focus: Two-Timescale Algorithms, Bilevel Optimization, Numerical Reformulations

    Bilevel optimization problems inherently encounter structural ill-posedness when lower-level problems admit non-unique solutions. This research investigates well-posed regularizations and two-timescale stochastic/deterministic approximation schemes to establish robust asymptotic convergence guarantees for hierarchical decision problems.

    Oral presentation at the 5th International Conference on Nonlinear Applied Analysis and Optimization (ICNAAO 2025).

    Lifting and Penalisation Approach for Mathematical Program with Vanishing Constraints (MPVC)
    Focus: Non-smooth Optimization, Exact Penalization, Constraint Qualifications

    Mathematical Programs with Vanishing Constraints (MPVC) frequently fail standard constraint qualifications (such as Mangasarian-Fromovitz CQ), severely challenging traditional KKT-based solvers. We study exact penalization and dimensional lifting techniques to formulate numerically tractable surrogate problems with strong stationary convergence properties.

    Presented at the 22nd Conference on Advances in Continuous Optimization (EUROPT 2025).

    Alternating Minimization for Least Square Estimation in Additive Semi-parametric Regression with Monotone Constraints
    Focus: Shape-Restricted Regression, Non-convex Optimization, Block Coordinate Descent

    Investigates shape-restricted additive non-parametric and semi-parametric regression under monotonicity constraints. Develops computationally efficient alternating minimization algorithms with global convergence guarantees for high-dimensional least-squares estimation.

    Presented at the 34th European Conference on Operational Research (EURO 2025).

    Projection-Based Iterative Algorithms for Eigenvalue Computation and Kaczmarz Methods for Sparse Reconstruction
    Focus: Randomized Row-Action Iterations, Sparse Regularization, Underdetermined Systems

    Examines randomized projection algorithms (such as the randomized Kaczmarz algorithm) for large underdetermined linear systems \( Ax = b \). Formulates regularized projection schemes designed to converge directly to sparse vectors with low per-iteration complexity, accompanied by study of projection-based eigensolvers.

    Theses & Dissertations

    Multiaxial Fatigue Life Prediction
    Master's Dissertation • Department of Civil Engineering, IIT Kharagpur (May 2017 – Apr 2018)
    Advisor: Prof. Arghya Deb
    Investigated the extension of the Highly Stressed Volume (HSV) approach to multiaxial fatigue analysis using the Finite Element Method (FEM). Validated the combined HSV–FEM framework for uniaxial fatigue, obtaining accurate life estimates, and identified structural limitations in multiaxial predictions despite parameter modifications.
    Stress Concentration in Nonaxisymmetric Rebars
    Bachelor's Dissertation • Department of Civil Engineering, IIT Kharagpur (May 2016 – Jul 2017)
    Advisor: Prof. Arghya Deb
    Investigated the increase in stress concentration beyond an optimal notch radius in rebars with asymmetric transverse ribs using finite element simulations. Developed analytical models in MATLAB using numerical integration and solid mechanics to study stress components at critical points, identifying the influence of shear stresses associated with cross-sectional area and shear-center variations.
