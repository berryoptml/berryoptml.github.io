    Anand Kumar is a doctoral research scholar and Prime Minister's Research Fellow (PMRF) in the Department of Industrial Engineering and Operations Research (IEOR) at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (IIT Bombay), supervised by Prof. K. S. Mallikarjuna Rao. His research focuses on mathematical optimization, with particular emphasis on bilevel optimization, two-timescale algorithms, and iterative projection algorithms for sparse reconstruction and eigenvalue computation. He is also interested in machine learning and computational mechanics. Previously, he worked as a Machine Learning Engineer at Quantiphi Inc. and an Airframe Intern at Airbus. He completed his Dual Degree (B.Tech and M.Tech) in Civil and Structural Engineering from IIT Kharagpur.

    Research Interests

    • Bilevel Optimization
    • Iterative Algorithms and Sparse Reconstruction
    • Machine Learning
    • Computational Mechanics and Structural Analysis
