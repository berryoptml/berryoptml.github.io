“People who wish to analyze nature without mathematics must settle for reduced understanding.”

I am a doctoral research scholar in the Department of Industrial Engineering and Operations Research at IIT Bombay, where my research was partly supported by the Prime Minister’s Research Fellowship. I am supervised by Prof. K. S. Mallikarjuna Rao. My research focuses on mathematical optimization, with particular emphasis on bilevel optimization, two-timescale algorithms, and iterative projection algorithms for sparse reconstruction and eigenvalue computation. I am also interested in machine learning and computational mechanics. Previously, I worked as a Machine Learning Engineer at Quantiphi Inc. and an Airframe Intern at Airbus. I received my bachelor’s degree from IIT Kharagpur.

Research interests

  • Bilevel optimization
  • Incentive design
  • Sparse reconstruction
  • Optimal control
