    Anand Kumar is a doctoral research scholar and Prime Minister's Research Fellow (PMRF) in the Department of Industrial Engineering and Operations Research (IEOR) at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (IIT Bombay).

    His research focuses on bilevel optimization, two-timescale algorithms, iterative projection algorithms and sparse reconstruction (such as Kaczmarz-based methods), and machine learning, with foundations in computational mechanics and structural analysis.

    “People who wish to analyze nature without mathematics must settle for reduced understanding.” — Richard P. Feynman

    Prior to his graduate research at IIT Bombay, he worked as a Machine Learning Engineer at Quantiphi Inc. (2018–2019) and completed a research internship at Airbus. He holds a Dual Degree (B.Tech + M.Tech) in Civil & Structural Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur (2018).

    Research Interests

    • Bilevel Optimization: Two-timescale algorithms, well-posed reformulations, and mathematical programs with vanishing constraints.
    • Iterative Algorithms & Sparse Reconstruction: Projection-based algorithms, randomized Kaczmarz methods, and eigenvalue computation.
    • Machine Learning: Representation learning, semi-parametric regression with monotone constraints, and alternating minimization.
    • Computational Mechanics: Finite element analysis (FEM), multiaxial fatigue life prediction, and structural damage detection.
