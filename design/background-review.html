“People who wish to analyze nature without mathematics must settle for reduced understanding.”

I am a doctoral research scholar and Prime Minister's Research Fellow (PMRF) in the Department of Industrial Engineering and Operations Research (IEOR) at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (IIT Bombay), supervised by Prof. K. S. Mallikarjuna Rao. My research focuses on mathematical optimization, with particular emphasis on bilevel optimization, two-timescale algorithms, and iterative projection algorithms for sparse reconstruction and eigenvalue computation. I am also interested in machine learning and computational mechanics. Previously, I worked as a Machine Learning Engineer at Quantiphi Inc. and an Airframe Intern at Airbus. I completed my Bachelor’s in Technology from IIT Kharagpur.

Research interests

  • Bilevel optimization
  • Incentive design
  • Sparse reconstruction
  • Optimal control
