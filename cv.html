    Research Interests

    Bilevel optimization; iterative algorithms and sparse reconstruction; machine learning; computational mechanics and structural analysis.

    Research Experience

    Indian Institute of Technology Bombay
    Graduate Research • Jan 2019 – Present
    • Research on two-timescale algorithms for bilevel optimization.
    • Study of projection-based iterative algorithms for eigenvalue computation and Kaczmarz-based methods for sparse reconstruction.
    • Seminar on the control of neutral-type time-delay systems arising in oil-well drilling vibrations.
    Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur
    Department of Civil Engineering • Graduate & Undergraduate Dissertations
    Multiaxial Fatigue Life Prediction (Master's dissertation • May 2017 – Apr 2018)
    Advisor: Prof. Arghya Deb
    • Investigated extending the highly stressed volume (HSV) approach to multiaxial fatigue analysis using the finite element method (FEM).
    • Validated the combined HSV–FEM approach for uniaxial fatigue, obtaining accurate life estimates; identified limitations in multiaxial predictions despite parameter modifications.
    Stress Concentration in Nonaxisymmetric Rebars (Bachelor's dissertation • May 2016 – Jul 2017)
    Advisor: Prof. Arghya Deb
    • Investigated the increase in stress concentration beyond an optimal notch radius in rebars with asymmetric transverse ribs, using finite element simulations.
    • Developed analytical models in MATLAB using numerical integration and solid mechanics to study stress components at a critical point.
    • Identified the influence of shear stresses associated with cross-sectional area and shear-center variations on the maximum principal stress.
    Structural Damage Detection Using Metaheuristics (Research internship • May 2016 – Jul 2016)
    Advisor: Prof. D. Maity
    • Implemented structural and eigenvalue analysis of three-dimensional frames in MATLAB, modeling damage through reductions in element elastic moduli.
    • Used a frequency response function (FRF)-based objective with ant colony optimization (ACO) and particle swarm optimization (PSO) to identify damaged elements.
